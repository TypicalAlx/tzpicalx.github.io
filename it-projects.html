StreamFinder

01

one app, every service

A mobile app prototype that lets you search every streaming service at once — so you spend less time hunting and more time watching.

Course
CAP 3104 · Group 8
Team
5 members
Tools
Figma, Google Forms
Type
Mobile app · UX research

The problem

Shows and movies are scattered across Netflix, Hulu, Max, Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV+ and more — with no way to browse them all at once. Titles hop between services as licensing deals change, so people waste time jumping app to app.

Who it’s for

College students and young adults juggling multiple subscriptions who want to find something to watch without switching apps — and want to browse, filter and organize based on the services they actually pay for.

The research

We interviewed 6 people in our target audience — 3 face-to-face and 3 through a Google Forms questionnaire — using identical questions so the answers could be compared reliably. The goal: learn what people weigh when deciding what to watch.

What’s in the prototype

  • Onboarding & service picker
  • Search across every service
  • Genre results
  • Filter by type, director & decade
  • Rotten Tomatoes & IMDb filters
  • Where-to-watch details
  • Saved list
  • Actor pages
  • Sign in & sign up
